# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique the work without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information and impersonation are not welcome.

Assume contributors have different backgrounds and levels of experience. Explain evidence and tradeoffs, preserve attribution, and make room for correction. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to keep collaboration safe and useful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers: use the private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact the repository owner through GitHub (<https://github.com/pgoode41>). Do not put sensitive reports in a public issue.
