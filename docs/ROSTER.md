## Reading this roster

A row is not a claim of universal coverage. `qualified_bounded` means the named identity executed with recorded evidence for the linked plan items only; new source, a new build or a different device never inherits it. Closing a family requires every `required` row of that family to be `qualified_bounded` at the family's acceptance layer and the remaining condition in `docs/progress.json` to be met; `deferred` rows are retained as unverified and are excluded from closure, not counted as passing.
